# muradL IPTV Player – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 17, 2026*

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how muradL IPTV Player ("the app"), developed by Murat Muradıl Mert ("we", "us"), handles
information when you use the app on Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. By using the app, you agree to the
practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate our own servers for the app and we do not require you to create an account. We do not collect,
store or sell your name, e-mail address, viewing history, playlists or login credentials.

- **Playlists and credentials:** M3U URLs, playlist files and Xtream Codes credentials you enter are stored only on
  your device (credentials in encrypted storage). They are sent only to the server you configured, never to us.
- **Local media:** If you choose to play local files, the app reads them only on your device. We do not scan, upload
  or share your files.
- **We do not provide content:** The app is strictly a media player. We do not provide, host or endorse any channels,
  streams or media.

## 3. Advertising (phones and tablets only)

On phones and tablets, the free version of the app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. **No ads are shown on
Android TV devices.** Ads appear only on browsing screens (menus and channel/movie/series lists) and never during
full-screen playback.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

- the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
- ad interactions (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can learn how Google uses this information at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Your choices:**

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** Users in these regions are asked for consent through Google's consent form
  before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Advertising ID:** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's
  Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

## 4. "Remove ads" purchase

You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed entirely by **Google Play**; we never receive
your payment card or billing details. The app only receives the purchase status from Google Play in order to turn ads
off and to restore your purchase on other devices.

## 5. Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

| Service | Purpose | Data processed |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob (incl. User Messaging Platform) | Showing ads, collecting consent | See section 3 |
| Google Play Billing | "Remove ads" purchase | Purchase status and purchase token |
| Firebase Remote Config | Turning ad formats on or off remotely | Firebase installation ID, app and device information |
| Google Cast | Casting a stream to your TV, only when you choose to | Stream address you cast, Cast device information |
| Google Play In-App Review | Optional rating prompt | Handled by Google Play |

Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> ·
Firebase: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## 6. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to stream media from the sources you add and to load ads.
- **Local network access:** to reach streams, UDP proxies and Cast devices on your home network.
- **Local files:** opened only through the Android file picker when you choose a file; the app has no general storage
  permission.
- **Advertising ID:** used by Google AdMob on phones and tablets (see section 3).

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Data security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our own systems. Data stored on your device remains there until you clear the
app's data or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google services is retained according to Google's policies.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
